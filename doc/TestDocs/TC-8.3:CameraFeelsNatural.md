# TC-8.3: Camera look around feels natural

**Test Description:** Open the game scene, move and look around while analyzing any lag or sudden jumps in objects or camera movement.  
**Module:** User Controls  
**Priority:** High  
**Pre-conditions:** Basic gameplay scene  

### Test Steps
1. Open the game to the 3d environment  
2. Look in a full circle clockwise and counterclockwise on the xz plane  
3. Look in a full circle up and down along the y axis  
4. Move the camera on diagonals (not a neat plane)  
5. Move the player forward, backward, left and right  
6. Repeat step 5 while moving the camera up and down  
6. Repeat step 5 while moving the camera left and right  
7. Repeat step 5 while moving the camera on diagonals  
8. Move the player in circles, left and right
6. Repeat step 8 while moving the camera up and down  
6. Repeat step 8 while moving the camera left and right  
7. Repeat step 8 while moving the camera on diagonals  
9. For each step note any visual glitches, objects moving out of step, or player lag along with which step caused it.  

### Expected Results
All objects should move coherently, the player should move at a speed and in a direction consistent with the buttons pressed and length of press, and no visual shearing should appear.
